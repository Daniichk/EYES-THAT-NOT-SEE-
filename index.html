Visualizing the Invisible

Mathematics isn't about numbers. It's about patterns and intuition.

01. The Derivative

The derivative measures the instantaneous rate of change. Imagine a car moving: the derivative is exactly what your speedometer shows at a single moment.

On this graph of f(x) = x², move your mouse to see the tangent line change its slope 2x.

02. The Essence of Pi

π is the ratio of a circle's circumference to its diameter. But it can also be seen as the limit of regular polygons.

Move your mouse left-to-right to increase the number of sides. Watch the perimeter converge to a perfect circle.

03. Linear Algebra

A matrix isn't just a grid of numbers; it's a transformation of space. It stretches, rotates, and squishes the coordinate system.

Interact with the grid to see how a vector v moves when the basis vectors are shifted.
