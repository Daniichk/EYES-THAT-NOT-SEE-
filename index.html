Journal of Digital Behavior & Psychological Well-being

Study: Sensory Micro-Interactions as a Method of Cortisol Regulation

Authors: Research Lab Alpha-01 | Date: 2024

Abstract: This research explores the "Digital Fidget" phenomenon. We hypothesize that low-stakes, repetitive motor tasks (micro-interactions) allow the prefrontal cortex to recover from high cognitive loads. Below are nine experimental modules designed to test sensory feedback and user relaxation levels.

M1: Haptic Feedback

M2: Rhythmic Expansion

Hold to simulate deep inhalation

M3: Ephemeral Input

M4: Kinetic Weight

Click to add mass

M5: Binary Sorting

M6: Spectrum Shift

M7: Attention Trace

M8: Linear Tension

M9: Wave Pattern

Methodology: Users are instructed to spend 30 seconds per module. Preliminary data suggests a 15% reduction in perceived stress after interaction with Module 3 and Module 9.
